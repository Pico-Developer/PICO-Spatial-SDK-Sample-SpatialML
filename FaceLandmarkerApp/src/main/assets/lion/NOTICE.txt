Lion dance head — original sample artwork

Authored for this repository in Blender 4.5.9 LTS and distributed as the
ready-to-load lion-dance.glb asset. Geometry, materials and the embedded weave
normal texture were created for this sample. No external model, image or
downloaded texture is incorporated.

Copyright 2026 PICO. Artwork is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International,
consistent with this repository's asset policy:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The model depicts a Southern Chinese lion dance costume head, with lacquered
ornament, fur trim, a forehead mirror and independently articulated jaw/eyes.
asset.json records axes, dimensions, rig pivots and the exported GLB hash.
