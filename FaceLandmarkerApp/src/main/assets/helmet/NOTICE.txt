Authored in Blender 4.5.9 LTS for this sample and distributed as a ready-to-load
GLB. The model has a crimson metallic crown and temple shell, a hinged gold
faceplate with backing and rim walls, a complete rear and side head shell,
compact hinge hardware and cool-white eye slits. Its 25% enlargement is baked
into the geometry and hinge. It has no neck,
collar or shoulder geometry. No downloaded model or texture is incorporated.

The facial aperture and faceplate backing use the sample's MediaPipe canonical
face reference. MediaPipe provenance and Apache-2.0 attribution are
retained in face-landmarker/pipeline/SpatialML/face-landmarker/MEDIAPIPE-LICENSE.txt
and provenance.json. opening-helmet.json records axes, rig, bounds and checksum.

The separately packaged damaged-helmet.glb retains its existing attribution in
DAMAGED_HELMET_LICENSE.md.
